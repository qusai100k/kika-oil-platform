# Recommendation Customer Guide

Customers generate product guidance from a READY skin assessment result. The result shows the status, config version, primary product, alternatives, deterministic explanation, safety notice, product links, and explicit add-to-cart buttons.

The customer must choose to add a variant to the cart. The platform does not automatically add, buy, reserve, or discount products.

Recommendations are guidance based on available approved/provisional information. They are not diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed suitability.

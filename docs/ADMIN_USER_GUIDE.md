# Admin User Guide

Sign in with an authorized development account and open `/admin`. The sidebar displays only permitted sections. Product editors manage catalog content and variants; order staff follow the offered next-status choices; stock adjustments require a signed quantity and reason; owners manage coupons, settings, analytics, and audit records.

All currency and business settings are provisional. Do not enter secrets, bank credentials, medical claims, or private specialist notes. Archive products rather than deleting history. Confirm manual payment only after independently verifying the transfer.

# Business Requirements

All requirements are draft until owner approval.

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-001 | Establish an independent Arabic-first digital presence outside Instagram. | MUST | Owner |
| BR-002 | Present approved products and brand information consistently and professionally. | MUST | Owner |
| BR-003 | Support direct commerce when the selected MVP includes checkout. | MUST | Owner |
| BR-004 | Reduce repetitive manual customer guidance without removing human escalation. | MUST | Owner + specialist |
| BR-005 | Provide controlled, explainable product guidance without medical diagnosis. | MUST | Owner + specialist + legal |
| BR-006 | Allow the business to manage catalog, stock, orders, content, and customers according to scope. | MUST | Owner |
| BR-007 | Support verified human specialist consultations in the later approved scope. | SHOULD | Owner + specialist |
| BR-008 | Collect useful sales and concern trends using minimized, appropriately consented data. | SHOULD | Owner + privacy reviewer |
| BR-009 | Preserve customer trust through accurate content, transparent safety boundaries, and clear policies. | MUST | Owner + reviewers |
| BR-010 | Permit future expansion to products, languages, countries, and providers without rebuilding the core. | SHOULD | Owner + technical lead |
| BR-011 | Define operational ownership for orders, payments, shipping, support, content, safety, and incidents. | MUST | Owner |
| BR-012 | Launch through a controlled beta with measurable success and stop criteria. | SHOULD | Owner |

## Business rules requiring owner confirmation

- Product types, variants/custom formulas, price, stock unit, preparation time, and reorder behavior.
- Guest checkout versus account-required checkout.
- Payment confirmation, stock reservation, cancellation, return, refund, failed delivery, and consultation policies.
- Who may publish product content or recommendation rules and whether dual approval is required.
- The MVP option and beta success measures.

## Success measures (draft)

The owner should select targets for: catalog completeness, inquiry reduction, order completion rate, fulfillment accuracy, assessment completion, safe-referral rate review, assessment-to-purchase conversion, support response time, and customer satisfaction. No numeric target is assumed in Phase 0.

# Assessment Safety Review

The experience repeatedly states it is not diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, or a suitability guarantee. Severe or worsening irritation and open wounds suggest qualified review. Unclear infection concern, prescription use, pregnancy/breastfeeding, and previous reaction request more information. These are development safeguards, not medical rules, and require specialist approval before commercial use.

## Closure Notes

- Referral results were browser-tested for safe language and contain no product, oil, diagnosis, treatment, or cure claim.
- Ready results explicitly state that product matching is a future Phase 7 capability.
- Needs-more-information results ask for correction without making a claim about the customer's skin condition.
- Safety rules remain provisional until approved by the business owner and a qualified specialist.

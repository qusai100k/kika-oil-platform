# Assessment Consent and Privacy

Consent is explicit and unselected by default. Version `assessment-demo-v1`, actor, purpose containing the assessment identifier, and timestamp are retained. Draft answers may be deleted; submitted snapshots are read-only pending final legal retention policy. Audit events store counts, versions, and result codes—not full answers or sensitive text. Provisional retention: drafts 30 days, submissions and consent until policy approval, audits 12 months; none is a final legal commitment.

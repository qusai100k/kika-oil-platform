# Assessment Customer Guide

Open `/skin-assessment`, sign in, read and accept consent, answer the structured draft, save, review, and submit. Drafts appear in Account -> Assessments and may be resumed or deleted. Submitted assessments are read-only and show Ready for future recommendation, Needs more information, or Specialist review suggested. None is a diagnosis or product recommendation.

## Closure Notes

- If the result asks for more information, the same assessment reopens and takes the customer back to the relevant section.
- If another tab saved a newer version first, the older tab is rejected with a stale-update notice.
- Submitted assessments cannot be edited; corrections are available only for the needs-more-information state.
- Phase 6 does not show AI recommendations, product matching, specialist booking, diagnosis, or treatment advice.

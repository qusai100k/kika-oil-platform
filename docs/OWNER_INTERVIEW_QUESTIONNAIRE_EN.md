# Business Owner Interview Questionnaire — English

Record the answer, approver, and supporting example/file. “Not decided” is valid. Safety and legal answers require the appropriate reviewer.

## 1. Brand information
1. What is the official Arabic and English name, and is it registered? 2. Which logo, colors, fonts, and images may be used? 3. What brand story and customer tone should the site use?

## 2. Current sales process
1. Describe a sale from first message to delivery. 2. Who answers, and what is the typical inquiry/order volume? 3. Which steps are repetitive, slow, or error-prone?

## 3. Products
1. What are the current products, sizes, prices, and stock? 2. Are products fixed or do customers select size/type/scent/strength? 3. Is approved imagery, description, and use guidance available for each?

## 4. Ingredients and safety
1. What is the exact ingredient list and who verifies it? 2. What allergies, warnings, contraindications, and interactions are known? 3. Who will approve safety information, claims, and referrals?

## 5. Product preparation
1. Are products pre-made or mixed per customer? Describe the steps. 2. For custom mixtures, how are quantities, approval, and batch recorded? 3. How are quality, storage, shelf life, and reorder controlled?

## 6. Customer consultation process
1. What questions are asked before selecting an oil? 2. When do you refuse guidance and refer to a professional? 3. Are answers retained, where, and who sees them?

## 7. Recommendation rules
1. How is each skin type/concern mapped to a product? 2. Which answers exclude a product or ingredient? 3. Who approves rules and how often are they reviewed?

## 8. Orders
1. Is an account required or should guest checkout be allowed? 2. Which order statuses are used? 3. When is stock reduced and who can change orders?

## 9. Payment
1. Which methods are used now and desired at launch? 2. How is manual payment verified and who may confirm/refund? 3. Which currency, taxes, and fees apply?

## 10. Shipping
1. From and to which countries/cities do you ship? 2. How are fee and delivery estimate calculated, and which carriers are used? 3. What happens for bad addresses, failed delivery, or damage?

## 11. Returns and refunds
1. When are cancellation, return, or exchange accepted? 2. Does policy differ for opened or custom products? 3. Who approves refunds, by what route and timeframe?

## 12. Human specialists
1. Who consults, with which qualifications and verification? 2. What duration, price, channel, and working hours apply? 3. What are cancellation/no-show rules and required customer information?

## 13. Privacy and customer data
1. What data is truly needed and for how long? 2. Who may see assessment answers and private notes? 3. How should access, correction, and deletion requests work?

## 14. Website content
1. Which launch pages are needed and who writes/approves them? 2. Do privacy, terms, shipping, returns, and disclaimer texts exist? 3. Is there permission to publish customer reviews/images?

## 15. Administration
1. Who uses the dashboard and for which tasks? 2. Who changes price, stock, content, rules, and roles? 3. Which actions need two approvals or a clear audit trail?

## 16. Reports and analytics
1. Which five figures matter weekly/monthly? 2. Should common skin concerns be reported, and to whom? 3. Which exports are needed and why?

## 17. Budget and timeline
1. What is the development and monthly-service budget range? 2. Is there a launch deadline and what may be deferred? 3. Who reviews and approves each phase?

## 18. Technical account ownership
1. Who owns domain, hosting, email, payment, and storage accounts? 2. Which official email registers them? 3. Who holds recovery methods and approves access?

## 19. Launch expectations
1. Do you prefer catalog, commerce, or commerce plus guided assessment? 2. How large is beta and how is success measured? 3. Which failures stop launch and who supports customers?

## 20. Future expansion
1. Are more products, countries, languages, or currencies planned? 2. Are bundles, subscriptions, loyalty, WhatsApp, or an app desired later? 3. Which desired feature is explicitly unnecessary for launch?

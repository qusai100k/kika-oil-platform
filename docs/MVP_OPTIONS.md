# MVP Options

## Comparison

| Dimension | A — Basic Catalog | B — Commerce | C — Commerce + Guided Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Included | Premium public site, catalog/details, approved content, WhatsApp/Instagram contact | A plus accounts, cart, checkout, orders, manual/COD payment, stock and admin order handling | B plus structured assessment, deterministic screening, explainable rules-based recommendations, referral |
| Excluded | Accounts, checkout, orders, assessment, recommendation, booking, AI | Assessment, recommendation, AI, booking unless separately scoped | Conversational AI, image analysis; full specialist booking unless added separately |
| Benefit | Fast validation of brand/content and improved discoverability | Moves sales into an owned, measurable workflow | Tests the core differentiator and reduces repetitive guidance |
| Main risk | Does not reduce manual order/recommendation workload much | Operational mistakes if fulfillment/payment rules are unclear | Highest safety/content burden; unsafe if rules are unapproved |
| Complexity | Low | Medium | High |
| Relative effort | Low | Medium | High |
| Required business information | Brand, catalog, verified product content, contact channels | A plus country, currency, prices, stock, address, payment, shipping, order/cancellation policies and staff | B plus approved assessment, compatibility, exclusions, referral rules, consent and safety reviewer |
| Best use case | Owner needs a credible web presence quickly or inputs are incomplete | Products are standard and owner wants direct orders | Guidance is central, products/rules are stable, and qualified review is available |
| Owner demo | Responsive Arabic catalog and contact journey | Complete test order and admin handling | Assessment scenarios producing safe recommendation/referral/no-result |

## Provisional recommendation

**Option C**, delivered as release increments A → B → C. This recommendation is provisional. If verified product mappings and safety/referral rules are not available, the launch boundary should stop at A or B; placeholder rules must never be exposed as real guidance.

## Approval questions

- Which outcome matters most in the first 90 days: presence, direct orders, or reduced consultation workload?
- Can the owner provide complete product/operations data for B?
- Is a qualified reviewer available to approve and maintain rules for C?
- What budget and beta date are acceptable?
